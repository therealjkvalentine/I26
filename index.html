What the I26 play fork changes

I26 is a reimplementation of Interstate '76 (1997) in Godot. This fork carries a stack of fixes and options on top of it; each section below is one part of that stack as merged into the fork's main, newest first, with the before and after pictures that were taken when it was made. Open a section to see it; click a picture to open it at full size. Some sheets set the original 1997 game beside I26 for comparison.

Built 2026-11-12 from main bc770d39. 123 sections (A to DT): 88 with pictures, 35 without.
