# Changelog

## Unreleased

Network games use protocol 23: this version does not play with 0.11.0 or earlier.

### New
- Map editor: switching between the editor and its playtest is much faster. Coming back to the editor is instant (it was a 2 to 3 second reload) because the editor is kept aside while you play, and going into the playtest takes about 1 second (1.6 s on the big T01 map in the modern look) instead of 2 to 3 (and more on a slow machine). An edit you make always shows in the playtest.
- Map editor: a playtest (F5, the PLAYTEST button) drops your car under the mouse cursor, on the ground, a road or a bridge's deck, facing the way the camera looks; with the cursor on the sky, off the map, on water, on a cliff or in a building, or off the 3D view, it goes where it used to, at the camera's pivot, and says why.
- Map editor: F12 switches between the modern and the 1997 look, as in a mission (F2 still works too).
- Map editor: the game's soundtrack plays while you edit, a random track after another at the Music level of Audio Control; a MUSIC button in the top bar turns it off and on, and a playtest hands over to the mission's own music.
- Bots use ramps: a bot whose car can reach the speed a ramp needs takes it as a shortcut across a gap, a canyon or a river when that is a clearly shorter way to its target, lining up on the ramp, holding its speed and keeping the wheel straight; a weak or damaged engine, or a ramp it cannot line up on, and it drives round as before.
- I26 tells you when a newer release is out: at start-up it quietly checks GitHub and shows "version X available" under the version in the corner of the game chooser and the main menu (click it to open the release page). Play Options (second page) has a Check for Updates row to turn it off (#138).

- The Developer panel has a second column: Settings, every key of shell.cfg's `[port]` without a menu row (docs/playing.md) as a button that steps it, saved at once and applied live where it can be; and Signals, the last three seconds of what goes to your devices (the wheel's force feedback, the pad's two motors, the telemetry feed's rate, pitch, roll, surge, sway and heave, and a bass shaker meter for when there is one), which can also stay over the game
- Graphic Detail is two rows shorter: HD Textures and HD Menu Art are keys in shell.cfg's `[port]` section (`hd_textures`, `hd_menu_art`, both on, so a pack is drawn in the modern look as soon as it is found; the 1997 look keeps the originals) and toggles on the Developer panel; a saved Off is kept
- The Developer panels (in a mission and on the main menu) scale with the window: twice the size from a 1080-pixel-high window, three times at 4K, so they read at high resolutions, and follow a resize.
- `tools/upscale-textures.py` matches the model's colours back to the original's (the broad colour and brightness of each area, keeping the model's sharper detail), so upscaled cars, signs and the cockpit no longer come out darker or tinted; `--from` applies it to a pack made earlier, `--no-colour-match` skips it, and the cockpit's pixel lettering keeps its own colours.
- In the modern look the cockpit's weapon and special labels are embossed tape lettering (the Impact Label font) instead of the plates' blocky painted pixels, sharp with or without a texture pack, in the plates' own spellings; the 1997 look keeps the painted plates.
- Graphic Detail's Hood Finish has three steps, Matte, Satin and Gloss, on the Esc menu's pad as well as the main menu's, and covers the cars' paint as well as the cockpit hood; the new default, Satin, is a step less shiny than before, so the sky no longer mirrors sharply across the hood. Gloss is the old look; a saved Matte stays Matte.
- Rounds striking the ground are drawn about a third fainter (less dust, dimmer and fewer sparks, a weaker flash) so they do not draw the eye as hits on cars do; and three keys in shell.cfg's `[port]`, `effects_size`, `effects_brightness` and `effects_opacity` (percent, 25 to 150, 100 as drawn), scale all of the modern look's weapon effects: tracers, impacts, muzzle flashes, rocket trails, smoke and explosions.

### Fixed
- The macOS build starts again: on macOS 26 it was killed as it opened ("The application cannot be opened", "Launchd job spawn failed"). Tried on an Apple-silicon Mac with macOS 26 (#144).
- Black parts of a car's paint (stripes, trim, grilles, tyres) may no longer look corrupt on some graphics cards (may fix the reported black car textures): the cars' per-part finishes are gone, every car is clear-coated paint all over with only the windows kept as glass.
- Memory use no longer climbs with every mission you start in one session (some 400 MB each in a big map): a finished mission's smoke and the force feedback no longer keep it alive.
- Memory use stays level when you restart a mission or go between the map editor and its playtest over and over: the cars' tail-lamp glow, a stray node per scene and the memory a freed scene left in the allocator's heap (10 to 20 MB every time) no longer pile up.
- The distant mountains no longer vanish behind a map on high ground in the modern look: a map made or edited in the map editor or built from the real world gets its ring of mountains placed once for the map, from its lowest ground, so they stand over the horizon wherever you drive and never shift as you move. Stock maps look as before. The 1997 look's horizon mountains stand as in the original, at every draw distance and in the map editor, sinking with the camera's height.
- Map editor: the 2 m wood ramp is placed the right way round on a road, climbing along the road instead of lying across it.
- Co-op: a gun emplacement that has been shot down now keeps its burnt shape as a solid obstacle for the other players' cars too (and for players who join afterwards), so a client no longer collides with the intact gun and gets snapped back.
- Co-op: a gate or roadblock the mission slides open (or shut) now moves for the other players' cars as well as for the picture, so a client no longer drives into an invisible gate at its old place and gets snapped back, a stutter when crossing it.
- Police sirens wail as in the original: once a mission turns a police car's siren on, it keeps sounding until the mission turns it off or the car is wrecked, instead of playing once, and the trip's and the scenarios' own police cars sound the siren rather than a short car horn, as on the 1997 CD. A Sheriff that honks at a car in its way turns its siren on too.
- Your car no longer hops at the start of a mission: it is already at rest on its suspension when the loading screen lifts, instead of dropping onto the ground in the first moments (#141).
- The menu music no longer cuts out for a moment after the loading screen: it plays on through the last part of the loading, and the mission's own track starts without a hitch (#142).
- In the modern look the ammunition counters' digits sit on the drums the panel paints, without the white column that ran over each counter's right-hand edge, and the rear-view mirror's frame and glass have smooth rounded edges instead of steps, with or without an HD texture pack; the 1997 look keeps the original's pixels.
- The melee arena lists (Instant Melee and the network host form's MELEE list) no longer show copies of the story missions that co-op packs put in addon (Scene 1 (Co-op), Oil Well Well (Scenario), ...): the fifteen arenas and your own maps stay, and the host form's CO-OP list has the missions.
- Story missions are listed by their scene number and what happens in them ("Scene 4: Dream Race", "Scene 5: Save Taurus") in the co-op list, the developer's scene picker, All Scenes bookmarks and the map editor, instead of titles the original game never shows that mostly do not fit the missions ("4. The Desert War", "5. Roswell"); Training keeps the main menu's name.
- The modern look's smoke no longer shows a fringe over the outline of a car or a hill in front of it: the smoke layer, drawn at half size, now takes the nearest surface under each of its pixels, not the one at its centre (#95).
- The network game's Esc menu says Leave Match ("Leave this match (back to the menus)?") and Exit I76 ("Quit Interstate '76 (back to the desktop)?") instead of Leave Game and Exit Game, which read alike; the single-player menu is unchanged.
- With an HD texture pack the cockpit's rear-view mirror has no cream box round its frame, the radar's blips, range lamp and contact LED are drawn where they belong, and switching HD Textures off or on in play (or flipping to the 1997 look) redraws the radar, weapon and damage panels, wheel and arms at the new size, the 1997 look always in the originals. `tools/upscale-textures.py` now scales the weapon and special labels, the ammunition digits and the mirror frame in their own colours (thin olive digits, even lettering, rounded corners) instead of through the model: run it again with `--from` over your pack after deleting those files (`zdue*`, `zdse*`, `znbe*`, `zmir*`).
- The dream is black and white again with an HD texture pack: the pack's upscales of the ground, sky and scenery the dream's palette turns grey are turned grey the same way, keeping their detail; the road and the cars keep their colour, as in the original.
- Held keys no longer stop working after Shift, Control or Alt lose their release outside the game (Control+F2 opening the menu bar on macOS, a system screenshot shortcut): the game lets such a key go at the next key press or mouse move, rather than every driving key acting only on its press until that modifier was pressed again.
- The 3D garage plays the menu track on, as the original's CONFIGURE CHASSIS does, instead of a boombox cycling through every track of the disc, the title and ending tracks included; the boombox is shell.cfg `[port] garage_radio=on` and plays only the mission tracks.
